What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored in text files that are saved on your computer or other devices when websites are loaded in a browser. They are widely used to remember you and your preferences, either for a single visit (through a "session cookie") or for multiple repeat visits (using a "persistent cookie").

Session cookies are temporary cookies that are used during the course of your visit to the Website, and they expire when you close the web browser.

Persistent cookies are used to remember your preferences within our Website and remain on your desktop or mobile device even after you close your browser or restart your computer. They ensure a consistent and efficient experience for you while visiting our Website or using our Services.

Cookies may be set by the Website ("first-party cookies"), or by third parties, such as those who serve content or provide advertising or analytics services on the website ("third party cookies"). These third parties can recognize you when you visit our website and also when you visit certain other websites.

Which cookies this website actually uses

This site sets no first-party cookies of its own, uses no analytics, and runs no advertising. Browsing the pages sets nothing on your device.

Two features embed a third party, and each is opt-in. Nothing from either is loaded until you choose it:

  • The booking calendar on the home and services pages is provided by Cal.com. It is not loaded until you press "Load the calendar". Once loaded it sets its own cookies (__Secure-next-auth.csrf-token, __Secure-next-auth.callback-url and __cf_bm), which it uses to run the booking form and filter automated traffic. If you would rather not load it, use the request form beside it instead — that form sets no cookies.
  • Video embeds on the videos page use YouTube's privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com mode, which does not set tracking cookies unless you actually play a video. Pressing play hands you over to YouTube, and Google's own cookie policy then applies.

Fonts are served by Bunny Fonts and the animation library by Cloudflare's open-source CDN. Neither sets cookies. Form submissions are handled by Formspree; see the Privacy Policy for what happens to the details you send.

Because nothing non-essential loads without you asking for it, this site does not show a cookie consent banner. Pressing "Load the calendar" or playing a video is the consent, and you can withdraw it by clearing cookies for this site in your browser.

What are your cookie options?

If you don't like the idea of cookies or certain types of cookies, you can change your browser's settings to delete cookies that have already been set and to not accept new cookies. To learn more about how to do this or to learn more about cookies, visit internetcookies.org
