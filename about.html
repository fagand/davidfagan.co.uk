Hi, I'm David.

Web developer, graphic designer & photographer based in Glasgow, Scotland. I've been building things on the internet since 2006.

I'm years old and I've always had a passion for computers and technology. When I was around 15, I built my first website — I designed the layout, sliced and coded it, secured hosting, and launched it. The site went on to have a period of great popularity, which only deepened my interest in the web.

That passion took me into further education where I graduated with a BSc in Computing from Glasgow Caledonian University. Since then I've continued building websites professionally and independently, always keeping a close eye on what's new in the industry.

Alongside development, I've always had a creative side. Graphic design came early — making layouts and signatures for web communities as a teenager. Photography came later and has grown into something I take seriously, whether it's landscapes, events, or portraits.

I have experience in Java and C#, and have built extensively on my web foundations with JavaScript, jQuery, PHP, and modern CSS. I care about the details — clean code, fast load times, and experiences that actually work on every device.

My work history and full CV are available on request — get in touch and I'll send it over.
